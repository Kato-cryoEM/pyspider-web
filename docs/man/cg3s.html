CG 3S - Center of Gravity - Solid body option

(6/25/96)

PURPOSE

Compute center of gravity and radius of gyration of a 3-D density distribution.

SEE ALSO

CG [Center of Gravity]
CG 3 [Center of Gravity - Radius of gyration]
CG PH [Center of Gravity - Phase approximation ||]

USAGE

.OPERATION: CG 3S [x],[y],[z],[rg]

.THREED FILE: 3DQ003
[Enter name of file containing the 3-D density distribution.]

THRESHOLD: 0.53 [Only data above the threshold will be used in computation.]

NOTES

  1. The coordinates of the center of gravity, with respect to the center at (NSAM/2 + 1, NROW/2 + 1, NSLICE/2 +1), and the radius of gyration are printed out and put into the first four (optional) register variables appearing on the operation line.

  2. For this operation, data above the threshold are replaced by a uniform density. Thus, the 3-D object is treated as a solid body without internal density variations.

  3. For the definition of the radius of gyration, see International Tables of Crystallography, vol. III, p327.

SUBROUTINES

CENGR3
