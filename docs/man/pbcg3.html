PDB CG3 - PDB - Calculate center of gravity

(9/16/06)

PURPOSE

To determine coordinates of gravity center of atomic coordinates in a PDB file.

USAGE

.OPERATION: PDB CG3 X11,X12,X13,X14,X15,X16,X17
[X11-X13 will receive center of gravity coordinates in PDB
cordinate system. X14-X16 will receive center of gravity coordinates in SPIDER coordinate system with unit
of Angstrom. X17 will receive the total number of counted
atoms. Also, the centers of gravity in both coordinate system
are printed out. ]

.INPUT FILE: aaa.pdb
[Enter the name of the PDB file with extension.]

NOTES

  1. The coordinates should be divided by pixel size if the coordinates are expected in the unit of pixel.

  2. Implemented by Zhong Huang, Pawel A. Penzcek.

SUBROUTINES

PDBCG3

CALLER

PDB
